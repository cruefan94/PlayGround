MISSION BRIEF

At 04:40, forward observers report NVA and Viet Cong elements moving through Ban Doc village toward Firebase Raven. Your platoon holds the firebase and its command bunker. The relief column is ten turns out.

Hold the bunker until relief arrives. Trade ground for time when you must. Use prepared positions, machine-gun suppression, armour, and the 105mm battery to break each assault before it reaches the wire.

HOW TO FIGHT

01 · SELECT

Click a friendly unit on the map, in the order of battle, or use keys 1–6.

02 · MOVE

Green hexes are reachable. Terrain costs vary; jungle and hills slow movement, paddies expose you.

03 · FIRE

Click an enemy inside your range. Firing ends the unit's action. Moved units may still fire; units that fire cannot move.

04 · END TURN

Press E or END TURN. Every surviving enemy then moves and fires. Hold for ten turns.

COMBAT NOTES

COVER

Jungle, villages and the firebase give +2 cover. Hills and elephant grass give +1. Cover reduces incoming damage. The bunker has reinforced cover.

SUPPRESSION

M60, M2, mortar and air attacks pin targets. Pinned units lose movement and firepower for one activation. Watch for the amber warning.

SUPPORT

105mm fire damages a hex and its neighbours — including friendlies. Huey runs hit and pin enemies only. Dustoff restores 7 HP.
